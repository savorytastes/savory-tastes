Before you ask.

For retailer, distributor, sampling, or press inquiries, email info@savorytastes.com.

What exactly is Masala-ish?

It is a crunchy rice-and-lentil snack stick seasoned with a warm blend of savory spices. The product is still in development, so final specifications may evolve before launch.

Is Masala-ish spicy?

The goal is flavorful warmth rather than overwhelming heat. Chili is part of the current spice blend, but it is not intended to dominate the snack.

Where will it be available?

Initial availability is expected to focus on direct sales, local sampling, and select retail partners. Join the launch list for updates.
