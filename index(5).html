Before you ask.

What exactly is Masala-ish?

Masala-ish is a crunchy rice-and-chickpea snack stick seasoned with a bold blend of classic masala spices. Final specifications may evolve before launch.

Is Classic Masala very spicy?

The goal is bold, layered savory flavor rather than overwhelming heat. Chili is part of the current blend, but it is not intended to dominate.

What product claims are planned?

The current formula is intended to be 100% plant based, gluten free, all natural, and made without added sugar, artificial flavors, artificial colors, or added preservatives. Final claims will be confirmed before production. The product contains sesame.

Is the package shown final?

It is the current package design direction. Nutrition information, ingredient order, claims, net weight, and certain production details may change before printing.

Where will it be available?

Initial availability is expected to focus on direct sales, local sampling, and select retail partners. Join the launch list for updates.

Do you offer wholesale or retail samples?

We welcome conversations with independent retailers and potential partners. Email info@savorytastes.com with your store or organization details.
